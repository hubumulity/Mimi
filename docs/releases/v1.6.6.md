## English

- On desktop, browse and search saved sessions, and export TXT, Markdown or a WAV for each recorded audio source. Stop live subtitles to play recordings, pause or seek.
- Desktop diagnostics separate the current state, recent errors and events. When audio recording is enabled, subtitle controls show its recording status.
- Desktop subtitles support 14–48 px text and a choice of all recent subtitles or the latest 1–10 entries. Scroll up to read earlier subtitles; the overlay also has a larger drag area along its top.
- Linux audio-source lists now show local application icons, with safer matching to avoid incorrect icons.
- Improved recovery from temporary Apple Speech interruptions and made recognition errors clearer.
- Fixed missing final subtitles when stopping custom speech recognition.

Update from Settings → Software Update, or download the installer below. Portable Windows users can replace their existing executable after quitting Mimi.

Automated checks and a short live Apple Speech / Apple Translation session passed. Long-session recovery, full upgrade installation and physical Windows, Linux and Android devices were not revalidated.

Thanks to @hubumulity for their first contribution, subtitle display limits, and @EmmetZ for Linux application icons.

## 中文

- 桌面端可浏览、搜索已保存记录，导出 TXT、Markdown 或各音源独立的 WAV 录音。停止实时字幕后，可回听录音、暂停或拖动定位。
- 桌面诊断页区分当前状态、最近错误和事件；开启录音后，字幕控制区会显示录音状态。
- 桌面字幕字号支持 14–48 px，可显示全部近期字幕或最近 1–10 条，上翻查看较早字幕；浮窗顶部的拖动区域也已扩大。
- Linux 声音来源列表支持显示本机应用图标，并改善匹配以避免显示错误图标。
- 改善 Apple Speech 临时中断后的恢复，并让识别错误提示更明确。
- 修复停止自定义语音识别时可能丢失最后一条已确认字幕的问题。

可在「设置 → 软件更新」中更新，也可下载下方安装包。Windows 便携版请先退出 Mimi，再替换原有程序。

自动检查及 Apple Speech / Apple Translation 真实短会话测试通过；本次未重新验证长时间会话恢复、完整升级安装及 Windows、Linux、Android 实体设备。

感谢 @hubumulity 首次贡献字幕显示条数功能，也感谢 @EmmetZ 带来的 Linux 应用图标。
